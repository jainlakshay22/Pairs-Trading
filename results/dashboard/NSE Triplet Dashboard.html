Key Findings

📊 Signal is real, not noise. 174 of 177 triplets pass the 1000-permutation shuffle test — the conditional expectation relationship is not achievable by random permutation of anchor returns.

📉 Regime-dependent, not structural. Zero triplets maintain significance in ≥60% of rolling 252-day windows. Relationships held in 2019–22 but most broke down post-2023 regime change (RBI rate hikes, post-COVID normalization).

🏭 KOTAKBANK most predictable. Appears as Stock_C in 13 of 34 validated triplets — consistently the "follower" in the NSE information hierarchy.

🔄 Sector rotation dominant pattern. Most significant triplets show Finance/Energy anchors predicting Finance followers — capital flow signals rather than momentum.
